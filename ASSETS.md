# Visual asset sources

- `public/assets/m9-knife.png`: M9 Bayonet Doppler Phase 2, 512×384 transparent render. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isaac-goldberg/cs2-images/main/skins/m9bayonet/thumbnails/Doppler%20Phase%202%20Light.png . Original pixels preserved. Counter-Strike assets belong to their respective rights holders.
- `public/assets/neverlose-logo.png`: community NL icon from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tickcount/.p2c-icons/main/neverlose.png . MIT license is retained in `public/assets/neverlose-logo-LICENSE.txt`. Saved as a visual reference, not displayed on the site.
- `public/assets/neverlose-logo.svg`: locally composed, monochrome vector interpretation of the NL initials based on the community reference. It is not represented as a verified current official brand asset. Replace this file with the official vector if one is supplied.
- `public/favicon.svg`: custom NL favicon.
- Fonts: Manrope and Space Grotesk, distributed through Fontsource with their bundled licenses. Served locally.
- Icons: Lucide, distributed through `lucide-react` under its bundled license.

Requested stylistic reference: https://driftparadise.ru/ . Direct access to that site and https://neverlose.cc/ returned HTTP 403 from the environment's egress proxy during development. This implementation follows the user's described visual direction; it does not claim a verified reproduction of the inaccessible reference.
